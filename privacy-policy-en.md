This policy explains how Rosi Reader handles the user data of anyone using the app. It complies with the Microsoft Store privacy description requirements and is intended to be published at a public URL (the policy link will be registered on the app's Microsoft Store page).

## 1. About the app
Rosi Reader is a book reader for Windows 11. It lets you add books (PDF, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, TXT) from folders or by dragging files, read offline, search text in the book (Ctrl+F), mark pages, save quotes and highlights, look up words in online dictionaries, manage a vocabulary, create shelves and collections, view reading statistics, and export/restore backups.

## 2. Data collected
We do not collect your personal data, or any data from third parties — not for analysis, marketing or any other purpose. No library data, reading progress, bookmarks, quotes/highlights, statistics or vocabulary data is sent outside your device.

What we store locally on your computer:

- **Library:** title, author, format, file path, cover (image in memory), progress, favorites, status ("want to read"/"reading"/"read"), added date.
- **Bookmarks and quotes/highlights:** book location, text, color, notes, page position.
- **Shelves and collections:** names and book membership.
- **Authors:** photos (images in memory) and favorites.
- **Vocabulary:** words and meanings you added.
- **Statistics:** minutes and days read (counted only while the book is open and the window is focused).
- **Settings:** theme, font, line height, zoom, warm light, TTS voice and speed, daily reading goal, interface language.
- **Profile:** name and photo, if you fill it in.

Book files stay in the folders they came from: the app keeps only the file path (except on Android, where imported books are copied into the app's storage).

## 3. Communications with the internet (app network traffic)
The app does not do telemetry, analytics, shares your library/reading/statistics with third parties, and makes no network calls other than dictionary lookups.

The only network traffic is the **online dictionary lookup**, when you use the app's dictionary:

- The word you look up is sent to Wiktionary, following the interface language: `pt.wiktionary.org`, `en.wiktionary.org`, `es.wiktionary.org`, `fr.wiktionary.org`, `de.wiktionary.org` or `it.wiktionary.org`. On the desktop app (Windows), the lookups go through the app (Tauri) with a fixed allow-list of URLs: only the dictionary domains above (plus `api.dicionario-aberto.net`, used only for the Portuguese language).

## 4. Cookies
The app does not use cookies. Data is stored in the browser's local storage (WebView2 localStorage) on your device.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Data stays on the device until you remove it:

- In the app itself: remove books, collections, bookmarks, quotes/highlights and vocabulary words.
- In the app itself: "Export backup" and then "Restore backup" — the app can create and restore copies of your data.
- Reinstalling the app: the local storage is removed with the reinstall.

You may request deletion of personal data by exercising your rights (section 8).

## 6. Children
The app is not directed to children. If you believe minors under 18 had access to data, or that someone collected minor data without consent, please contact us.

## 7. Changes to this policy
This policy may be changed. The current version will be available at the policy link listed on the app's Microsoft Store page, and it will be updated when there are relevant changes to the data processed.

## 8. Data subjects' rights
According to the applicable law (LGPD in Brazil, GDPR in the EU), you have rights over your personal data: access, correction, deletion, limitation, portability and objection. You can exercise them through the Contact above or through the app's deletion and backup export functions.
